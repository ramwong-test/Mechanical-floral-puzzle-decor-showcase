Assembly Rules

  • Each slot on the pot accepts the base of one stem.
  • Type-A stem: the head (top) only accepts a flower or lotus leaf — that is the end of the chain.
  • Type-B stem: the head only accepts a connector; a connector can take the next stem on top, so a chain can keep growing.
  • Leaf connectors (Tulip, Mum, Rose) also count as connectors — a leaf grows at the joint.
  • Every piece can spin: angle the stems' curves and the leaves freely.
